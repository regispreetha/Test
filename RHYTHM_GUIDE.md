## 🎵 HOW TO USE RHYTHM CONTROLS

### What You Should See:

After the Record/Play buttons, you should see a **pink/coral section** that says:

**"⏱️ Rhythm & Tempo"**

With:
1. **Tempo slider** - Drag to change BPM (40-200)
2. **Duration buttons** - Click to select note length:
   - 🎵 1/4 (Quarter)
   - 🎵 1/2 (Half)
   - 🎵 1 (Whole)
   - 🎵 2 (Double)
   - 🔇 Rest

## 🎸 TRY THESE EXAMPLES:

### Example 1: Simple Spaced Chords
1. Type in chord input: `C:1 Am:1 F:1 G:1`
2. Click "Add to Sequence"
3. Click "Play"
4. You'll hear 4 chords, each 1 beat apart

### Example 2: Different Spacing
1. Type: `C:2 Am:0.5 F:0.5 G:1`
2. Add to sequence
3. Play
4. C plays for 2 beats, Am and F quick, G for 1 beat

### Example 3: With Rests (Silence)
1. Type: `C:1 REST:1 Am:1 REST:1 G:2`
2. Add to sequence
3. Play
4. You'll hear: C → silence → Am → silence → G (long)

### Example 4: Change Tempo
1. Move the **Tempo slider** to 60 BPM (slow)
2. Type: `C:1 Am:1 F:1 G:1`
3. Play - SLOW!
4. Move slider to 180 BPM (fast)
5. Play again - FAST!
